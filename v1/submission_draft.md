# Player Designed Island Competition Entry: The Isle of Skrap

## Concept Overview
**Name:** The Isle of Skrap (Districts: **Flotsheim** & **Jettstrom**)
**Theme:** A "Hub" Island built entirely from shipwrecks and ocean debris, inhabited by two rival Fremennik clans.
**Vibe Keywords:** Chaotic, Bustling, Salty.
**Location:** The **Northern Sea** (North-East of Neitiznot/Jatizso).
**Lore Justification:** The island sits atop a massive **Hydrothermal Vent** (underwater volcano) in the center of a northern gyre. This natural trap catches debris from Rellekka's trade routes and shipwrecks drifting from the Wilderness.

## The Pitch
Every Sailor knows that the sea gives, and the sea takes. But where does it all *go*?
Welcome to **Skrap**: a festering, floating, thriving shantytown built upon centuries of driftwood, coral, and the hulls of a thousand unfortunate ships snagged on the volcanic jagged rocks.

The island is a singular landmass (approx 120x120 tiles, dense and vertical), divided by a philosophical feud:
*   **Flotsheim (The Heights):** *"Nay! Keep it!"* The hoarders who build upwards.
*   **Jettstrom (The Docks):** *"Ya! Toss it!"* The traders who use the thermal vent to power their industry.

## Layout & Aesthetics
The island is a dense, "C" shaped atoll formed around the central **Steam Vent**.
*   **Flotsheim (North):** A vertical favela. Ships are stacked 4-5 stories high. There is zero wasted space; every inch is a walkway, a rope ladder, or a home.
*   **Jettstrom (South):** Industrial pontoon docks that harness the heat of the vent.
*   **The Steam Stream (Center):** The central volcanic vent, covered by a massive metal grate.

## Hub Utility & Timeless Features
1.  **The Steam Stream (High Alch/Incinerator):**
    *   *Concept:* The central hydrothermal vent releases superheated steam pressure.
    *   *Function:* Players toss unwanted items onto the Grate. The intense heat/pressure destroys them (High Alch value) or blasts them into the deep-sea currents to be "recycled" elsewhere in the game world.
    *   *Visual:* A bubbling, steaming vortex in the center of the island.
2.  **The Jettstrom Recycler:** A crusher powered by the steam pressure. Turns finished items back into raw resources.
3.  **The Bank (Flotsheim):** Located inside a massive, hollowed-out Leviathan Skeleton that spans the upper peaks of the ship-stack.
3.  **Salvage Contracts (The Daily Hook):**
    *   *Function:* The Flotsheim Quartermaster offers daily contracts to retrieve specific "driftwood" or "salvage" items found while Sailing.
    *   *Reward:* Unique untradeable bait, cosmetic upgrades for ships, or "Scrap" currency. This ensures players have a reason to visit every day.
4.  **The "Washed-Up" Spirit Tree:** A Spirit Tree sapling that grew in a pile of compost on a shipwreck. Provides instant high-level travel access to the open ocean.

## Lore & Quest: "One Man's Trash"
*   **Requirement:** Sailing Level 50.
*   **Story:** The Flotsheim clan has "rescued" a shipment of "Ancient Relics" (actually rusty anchors) that the Jettstrom clan was trying to throw away. The weight of the hoard is causing the island to sink on one side.
*   **Task:** The player must mediate the dispute, stabilize the island using Sailing skills (winches/ropes), and ultimately convince them to compromise: Keep the shiny stuff, toss the heavy stuff.

## Activities
1.  **Crane Operations (Construction/Strength):** A repeatable activity where players operate the massive Jettstrom cranes to load/unload trade ships.
    *   *Reward:* Construction XP and "Scrap" currency. (Note: No Sailing XP is granted).
2.  **The Stream Incinerator (Magic/Prayer):** A massive vent in Jettstrom. Players can toss unwanted items into the "Stream" to be carried away (High Alch/Offering function).
3.  **Wreck-Diving (Thieving/Hunter):** Dive beneath the island to pick pockets of "Fresh Meat" (newly crashed ships) before the Flotsheim clan claims them.

## Audio Vibe
*   **Tracks:** "Driftwood Drifters", "Tide of Debris", "Scrap Shanty".
*   **Vibe:** Accordions, creaking wood foley, seagulls, and a rhythmic "heave-ho" chant.

## Why it Fits OSRS (Dev & Design Notes)
*   **Asset Reuse:** 90% of the island utilizes existing assets (Shipwrecks, Port Piscarilius Cranes, Fremennik Buildings), minimizing Art Team workload.
*   **Engine Friendly:** "Verticality" is achieved using standard Z-planes (0-3) and bridges, not complex geometry.
*   **Grind Optimization:** The layout is designed for "Tick-Perfect" efficiency. A Deposit Box is located next to the Recycler/Incinerator to create a tight 10-tile gameplay loop, respecting the player's time and encouraging long-term engagement.
*   **Chunk Alignment:** The entire island footprint fits within a standard 2x2 Map Chunk configuration (128x128 tiles), easily placeable in the open ocean.
