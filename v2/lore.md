# Isle of Skrap: Lore & Ecology

## The Setting: The Great Gutter
The **Isle of Skrap** is not a natural island. It is a massive, calcified floating trash-barge located in a "dead zone" of the Northern Sea where several major currents collide. These currents bring driftwood, shipwrecks, lost cargo, and debris from all over Gielinor to a single swirling point.

Beneath this swirling vortex lies an underwater volcanic vent known as **The Belch**.

## The Two Clans
The population consists of Fremennik outcasts, drifters, and exiles who found the island. Over time, they split into two distinct factions based on how they view the trash.

### 1. Floatsheim (The Hoarders)
*   **Location:** The "Low Wards" and the central pile. They live close to the water and deep inside the mountain of trash.
*   **Philosophy:** "The Sea Provides." To throw something away is to insult the ocean. Every rusty nail, rotten plank, and heavy anchor is a gift.
*   **Role:** The **Ballast**. They build dense, heavy, and chaotic structures.
*   **Personality:** Superstitious, dirty, physically strong (from hauling junk), obsessed with "Weight."

### 2. Jettstrom (The Traders/Recyclers)
*   **Location:** The "High Docks" and the outer rim. They build cranes, catwalks, and processing plants on top of the Floatsheim pile.
*   **Philosophy:** "Turn Trash to Treasure." Debris is useless unless it is refined, sorted, and sold back to the mainland.
*   **Role:** The **Buoyancy**. They maintain the pumps, the trade ships, and the economy.
*   **Personality:** loud, mercantile, mechanic-savvy, obsessed with "Flow."

## The Symbiosis: Why They Need Each Other
They hate each other, but murder means mutual suicide. Here is the strict co-dependency:

### 1. Structural Survival (Ballast vs. Buoyancy)
This is the most critical reason. The Isle is floating on a volatile ocean.

*   **Why Traders need Hoarders:** Without Floatsheim piling up heavy heavy scrap (anchors, lead, stone) at the center/bottom, the island would be **top-heavy**. The massive cranes and machinery of the Traders would cause the island to flip over (capsize) in a storm. Floatsheim provides the **Keel/Counter-weight**.
*   **Why Hoarders need Traders:** Floatsheim just keeps adding weight. Without the Jettstrom engineers maintaining the **Steam-Pumps** and **Pontoon Systems** (powered by the vent), the Hoarders would eventually sink the entire island into the abyss.
    *   *Summary:* Hoarders stop the island from flipping; Traders stop the island from sinking.

### 2. The Vent ("The Belch") Power Dynamic
The underwater volcanic vent releases massive amounts of heat and steam.

*   **Floatsheim's Use:** They built their homes directly over the vent's opening (The "Core"). They use the passive heat to survive the freezing northern winds. They "cork" the steam with their junk pile.
*   **Jettstrom's Use:** The Traders tapped into this pressure. They need the steam pressure to run the **Recycler Crushers** and **Cranes**.
*   **The Dependency:** The Hoarders *are* the pressure valve. If Hoarders hoard too much, the pressure builds and pipes burst up top. If Traders siphon too much power, the Hoarders freeze. They have to balance the pressure together.

### 3. The Supply Chain (Raw vs. Refined)
*   **The Retrieval:** The currents around the isle are lethal. Only the maniacs in Floatsheim are crazy enough to dive in and "catch" the debris with hooks and nets. They are the resource gatherers.
*   **The Export:** Floatsheim has no interest in money (gold is just shiny weight to them). They trade the "boring stuff" (wood, standard iron) to Jettstrom in exchange for "rare heavy things" Jettstrom imports (like lead weights or marble statues). Jettstrom refines the raw trash and sells it to Rellekka/Keldagrim.
*   **Food:** Jettstrom ships bring in food. Floatsheim catches fish. Neither has enough alone.

## The Conflict (The Quest Hook)
The system works, but it is fragile.
*   **The Problem:** The Floatsheim Hoarders have found a "cache" of ancient, massive Dwarven Anchors. They believe these are holy relics from the Sea God.
*   **The Consequence:** They are dragging so many anchors onto the pile that the **Ballast** is becoming too heavy for the **Buoyancy**.
*   **The Threat:** The Jettstrom pumps are burning out trying to keep the island afloat. The water level is rising. The Traders are screaming that the island is sinking, but the Hoarders refuse to let go of their "Holy Heavy Ones."

## Cultural Flavor (OSRS Style)
*   **Floatsheim Greeting:** "May your boots be heavy." (Wishing you don't get blown away).
*   **Jettstrom Greeting:** "Keep the flow." (Time is money).
*   **The "Mayor":** There isn't one. There is a **Town Council**, but it's just a shouting match between the *Grand Scavenger* (Floatsheim) and the *Trade-Baron* (Jettstrom) separated by a thick glass wall because they spit when they argue.
