# Quest: The Sinking Feeling

## Overview
*   **Difficulty:** Intermediate (Requires completion of *The Fremennik Trials*).
*   **Length:** Medium (30-45 minutes).
*   **Quest Series:** Fremennik / Skrap.
*   **Reward:** Access to The Recycler, 10,000 Agility XP, Skrap Isle Teleport.

## Synopsis
The Isle of Skrap is tilting dangerously. The Jettstrom Traders blame the Floatsheim Hoarders for adding too much weight. The Hoarders blame the Traders for "weak pumps." The player must intervene before the entire island capsizes.

---

## Quest Walkthrough

### 1. The Listing Deck
*   **Start:** Speak to **Trade-Baroness Helga** (Level 1, Jettstrom Deck).
    *   *Camera Shake:* The screen shakes every 30 seconds.
    *   *Dialogue:* "Can't you feel it? We're listing 15 degrees Starboard! Those maniacs down below have dragged something massive onto the pile!"
    *   *Task:* Go down to Level 0 and find out what Grog is hoarding.

### 2. The Heavy One
*   **Location:** Level 0, The Heap (Bank Area).
*   **NPC:** **Grand Scavenger Grog**.
    *   *Observation:* Grog is sitting on a massive, golden, barnacle-encrusted anchor that is clearly too heavy for the trash pile.
    *   *Dialogue:*
        *   Player: "Grog, that anchor is sinking the island."
        *   Grog: "Nonsense. This is 'The Heavy One.' It is a gift from the Sea. It stays."
        *   Player: "If the island sinks, the anchor sinks too."
        *   Grog: "The Sea gives, the Sea takes. I am not moving it."

### 3. The Engineering Solution
*   **Task:** Return to Helga. She realizes Grog won't budge.
*   **Solution:** Speak to **Sparky** (Level 1, Engineer).
    *   *Sparky:* "We can't move Grog... but maybe we can move the center of gravity?"
    *   *Plan:* If they can't remove the anchor, they can *lower* it through the moon-pool (the Vent shaft) to hang *under* the island. This acts as a keel, stabilizing the tilt.

### 4. The Heist (Agility/Thieving)
*   **Challenge:** You need to attach heavy chains to the anchor without Grog noticing.
*   **Action:**
    1.  Distract Grog by throwing "Shiny Junk" (collected from nearby) to the other side of the pile.
    2.  Use **Agility** to climb the Trash Spire above the Bank.
    3.  Drop the Jettstrom Chains (from the Crane) onto the anchor.

### 5. The Boss: The Trash Golem
*   **Twist:** As the crane begins to lift the anchor (with Grog still on it), the disturbance awakens a **Trash Golem** formed from the disturbed debris.
*   **Fight:**
    *   *Level:* 85.
    *   *Mechanics:* Throws "Junk" (Range) and uses "Sludge Wave" (Magic).
    *   *Weakness:* Fire (lead it to the Vent steam vents).

### 6. Conclusion
*   The Golem is defeated. The Crane lowers the anchor through the floor into the water below.
*   The island stabilizes (screen shake stops).
*   **Grog:** "I... I am flying! The Heavy One pulls me down, but the island holds me up! It is a miracle!" (He is just dangling in the moon pool).
*   **Helga:** "Pressure is stable. Good work."
