# Player Designed Island Competition Entry: The Isle of Skrap

## Concept Overview
*   **Name:** The Isle of Skrap
*   **Theme:** A "Hub" built from shipwrecks and debris, split into two rival clans living on tethered floating platforms.
*   **Location:** Northern Sea (Hydrothermal Vent Gyre).
*   **Vibe:** *Waterworld* meets *Fremennik*. Chaotic, loud, and constantly steaming.

---

## The Pitch
Welcome to **Skrap**: where the ocean throws its garbage, and the Fremennik throw a party.
The island is not one solid landmass, but **two massive floating platforms** tethered together over a volatile underwater volcano (The Vent).

### The Two Factions
1.  **Floatsheim (West Platform):** *"The Hoarders."*
    *   They believe the trash is a gift from the sea. They build *upwards*, stacking ship hulls into unstable towers.
    *   **Role:** The Ballast. They provide the weight to keep the structures stable in the waves.
2.  **Jettstrom (East Platform):** *"The Traders."*
    *   They believe trash is profit. They build *outwards*, constructing industrial pontoons and cranes to salvage and export materials.
    *   **Role:** The Buoyancy. They maintain the steam pumps (powered by the Vent) to keep the hoarders from sinking everyone.

---

## Hub Utility (Why Players Visit)
This is designed to be a mid-level hub (Sailing Lv 50) with unique, permanent utility:

1.  **The Jettstrom Recycler:**
    *   *Function:* A "Reverse Anvil." Players can use metal items on it to break them down into raw bars/ore (at a loss, e.g., 50% yield).
    *   *Use Case:* Ironmen clearing junk, or converting drops into smithing XP supplies.
2.  **The Steam Stream (The Vent):**
    *   *Function:* Infinite Fire Source (Cooking).
    *   *Bonus:* "Steam Cooking" reduces burn chance for fish. Located in the underwater glass tunnels (Level -1).
3.  **The Agility Course:**
    *   *Route:* Starts at the sea-level docks, climbs the Floatsheim trash-spire, crosses the high-wire cranes of Jettstrom, and dives into the Vent.
4.  **The Bank:**
    *   Located inside the ribcage of a massive Leviathan skeleton on the Floatsheim side.

---

## The Quest: "The Sinking Feeling"
*   **Requirement:** Sailing Lv 50, *The Fremennik Trials*.
*   **Premise:** The island is tilting. The Jettstrom engineers claim the Hoarders have added too much weight.
*   **The Conflict:** Grand Scavenger Grog (Hoarder Leader) has found a "Golden Anchor" and refuses to throw it back. Trade-Baroness Helga (Trader Leader) is threatening to cut the tethers and let Floatsheim sink.
*   **The Solution:** The player must use **Sailing** (Winches) and **Construction** to build a rig that lowers the anchor *beneath* the island, turning it from a burden into a stabilizer (Keel).
*   **Boss:** The "Trash Golem", created when the disturbed junk pile fights back.

## Why This Fits OSRS
*   **Scope:** Fits perfectly within a 2x2 Chunk area.
*   **Assets:** Reuses existing shipwreck, dock, and Fremennik assets.
*   **Gameplay Loop:** The Recycler and Vent offer unique utility without breaking the game economy (it's a resource sink, not a faucet).
