# NPCs of the Isle of Skrap

## 1. Trade-Baroness Helga (Jettstrom Leader)
*   **Location:** The Upper Deck (Level 1).
*   **Visuals:**
    *   Wearing: Blue Fremennik robes modified with brass gears and a toolbelt.
    *   Holding: A large wrench and a clipboard.
    *   Animation: Constantly pacing, looking at gauges.
*   **Personality:**
    *   Stressed, bureaucratic, practical.
    *   Refers to the island as "The Platform" (refuses to call it an island).
*   **Dialogue Sample:**
    *   "Keep moving! If the weight distribution shifts by 2%, we'll all be fish food!"
    *   "Those savages below... they think trash is holy. I think it's just unrefined profit."

## 2. Grand Scavenger Grog (Floatsheim Leader)
*   **Location:** The Heap (Level 0, Bank).
*   **Visuals:**
    *   Wearing: A suit of armor made from mismatched pieces (Rune platebody, Bronze legs, bucket helmet).
    *   Holding: A jagged piece of driftwood as a scepter.
    *   Animation: Sitting on a pile of chests, occasionally polishing a rusty spoon.
*   **Personality:**
    *   Fanatical, superstitious, filthy.
    *   Believes the "Sea Gods" send specific trash for specific reasons.
*   **Dialogue Sample:**
    *   "Don't step there! That is a Holy Boot! It washed up last Tuesday!"
    *   "The Sky-People (Jettstrom) want to sell our gifts. Blasphemy. We keep. We hold. We sink... if we must."

## 3. Sparky (The Engineer)
*   **Location:** The Crane Control Room (Level 1).
*   **Visuals:**
    *   Gnome (surprisingly).
    *   Wearing: Welding goggles and an apron.
*   **Role:**
    *   Maintains the Recycler.
    *   Gives the Agility Course tutorial.
*   **Lore:** Was shipwrecked here, fixed a pipe, and they made him Chief Engineer because he was the only one who knew what a "wrench" was.

## 4. Old Man Barnacle
*   **Location:** The Fishing Docks (Level 0).
*   **Visuals:**
    *   Covered in seaweed.
    *   Skin is green/grey.
*   **Role:** Slayer Master (Task-only).
*   **Tasks:**
    *   *Scrap Crabs* (Crabs with metal shells).
    *   *Vent Eels*.
    *   *Rust Monsters*.
