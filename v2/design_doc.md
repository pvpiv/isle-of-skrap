# Isle of Skrap: Master Design Document (Revision 2)

## 1. The "Hub" Concept
A floating "Twin-Island" hub in the Northern Sea, formed by debris trapped in a gyre above a volcanic vent.

### Topology: The Split Atoll
The island is NOT a solid landmass. It is two separate floating platforms tethered together over a central volcanic vent.
*   **West Platform:** **Floatsheim (The Hoarders)**. Built from shipwreck hulls, erratic, vertical, organic.
*   **East Platform:** **Jettstrom (The Traders)**. Built from flat pontoons and metal grates, industrial, organized.
*   **The Center (The Gutter):** A channel of water separating them.
    *   **The Vent:** Directly in the middle of the Gutter.
    *   **The Tethers:** Massive chain-bridges and winches connecting West and East, keeping them from drifting apart.

---

## 2. Facilities & Locations
*   **The Thermal Vent (Center):**
    *   The "Steam Stream" Incinerator (High Alch/Sacrifice).
    *   Underground: Glass tunnels leading to the magma core (Cooking).
*   **Floatsheim (West):**
    *   **The Bank:** Inside a hollowed-out whale skeleton.
    *   **The Pile:** A chaotic jumping puzzle (Agility shortcut to upper levels).
*   **Jettstrom (East):**
    *   **The Recycler:** Industrial crusher for un-noting/breaking items.
    *   **The Cranes:** Activity for loading ships.

---

## 3. The Symbiosis (Revised)
*   **Tension:** The two islands are literally pulling away from each other due to currents.
*   **Connection:** They are held together by the **Master Winch** (at the Vent).
*   **Conflict:** Floatsheim is getting too heavy (Anchors). Jettstrom is pulling too hard (Industrial Winches). If the chain snaps, both islands drift into the open ocean and die.

---

## 4. Map Scale
*   **Total Footprint:** 128x128 Tiles (2x2 Chunks).
    *   Floatsheim: ~40x80 tiles.
    *   Jettstrom: ~40x80 tiles.
    *   Gutter/Vent: ~20 tiles wide in the center.
